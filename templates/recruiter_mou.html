MOU Terms & Conditions
  1. Purpose: This MOU governs the company’s use of HireHub recruitment services for job posting, candidate evaluation, and hiring activities.
  2. Authenticity of Information: The company confirms that all profile details, legal documents, representative details, and declarations submitted on the platform are true and current.
  3. Lawful & Fair Hiring: The company agrees to maintain fair, non-discriminatory, and legally compliant hiring processes, and avoid misleading job advertisements.
  4. Job Posting Integrity: Roles, eligibility, compensation, and responsibilities must be reasonably accurate and updated when circumstances change.
  5. Candidate Data Privacy: Candidate data accessed through the platform must be used only for recruitment and handled with confidentiality and security controls.
  6. Communication Responsibility: The company agrees to provide timely updates to candidates and avoid prolonged non-responsive hiring cycles.
  7. Policy Compliance: The company agrees to follow all current and future platform policies, moderation decisions, and verification requirements by HireHub.
  8. Audit & Verification Rights: HireHub may request additional documents or re-verification in case of policy, legal, or safety concerns.
  9. Breach & Enforcement: Any misuse, fraud, impersonation, or harmful conduct may result in suspension, account termination, and legal escalation where required.
  10. Digital Consent: The company representative’s digital signature confirms acceptance of this MOU and creates a traceable, time-stamped consent record.
